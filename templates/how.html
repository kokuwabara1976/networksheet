What do you know about them?

Do you know anything meaningful about each of the following categories? They may seem like relevant questinos, but it is helpful to think about how much you have invested in getting to know each person.

  • Friends: Do you know anything about their best friends?
  • Family: Do they have siblings or children? Where do their parents live?
  • Education: What is their highest degree? Where did they get their education?
  • Cultural background: Where and how did they grow up?
  • Interests: How do they spend their free time? What do they like to talk about outside of work?
  • Food: What do they enjoy eating? Do they have favorite restaurants or dishes?
