Understanding Your Network

From getting hired to getting things done and getting ahead, hardly anyone disputes the importance of “who you know” in business. Yet, an extensive body of research has shown that not all networks are created equal. A good network can accelerate your career, but many of us end up building networks that are ineffective at best, counterproductive at worst.

This online worksheet is designed to help you think more carefully about what your network looks like and how you manage professional relationships. You will be asked to name people from your core professional network, think about your relationships with them, and draw your network.

This worksheet takes about 15-30 minutes to complete, although there is no time limit. At any point in the worksheet, you may return to a previous page to change your responses as you notice something new or gain new insights. The entire worksheet is anonymous; no data will be recorded.
