NETWORKsheet: Understanding Your Network


From getting hired to getting things done and getting ahead, hardly anyone disputes the importance of “who you know” in business. Yet, a growing body of research has shown that not all networks are created equal. A good network can accelerate your career, but many of us end up building networks that are ineffective at best, counterproductive at worst.

This online worksheet is designed to help you think more carefully about what your network looks like and how you manage professional relationships.

This worksheet is anonymous. No data will be recorded.
