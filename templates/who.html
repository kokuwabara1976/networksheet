Who's in your network?

Below are questions to help you identify people in your network, people who have played an important role in your professional pursuits and development in the last 1-2 years. Please use initials to identify each contact (e.g. KK, LNS). You may enter each contact multiple times as needed.

Technical expertise

Name up to 3 people you go to for technical or operational advice and solutions. These people help you solve problems and get things done efficiently or creatively because of their expertise.

Information

Name up to 3 people you go to for information about new opportunities, events, or happenings at work or in your profession.

Political Support

Name up to 3 people you rely on to help you secure resources, positions, or opportunities to do new things or meet new people because of their role or influence.

Guidance

Name up to 3 people you go to for wisdom and feedback to help you grow professionally and personally. You consider them mentors to you.

Inspiration

Name up to 3 people you go to for inspiration (energy, focus, ideas) at work. They inspire, challenge, or encourage you to strive toward important goals.

Friendship

Name up to 3 people who have been the most important peers. You can discuss personal or non-professional matters with them. They give you emotional support and make you feel accepted.
