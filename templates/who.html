Who's in your network?

Below are questions to help you identify different types of people who have played an important role in your professional pursuits and development in the last 1-2 years. Please use initials to identify each contact (e.g. MM or Mike M; beware of people with the same initials). You may enter each contact multiple times as needed. If you are a full-time student, think about contacts from your previous job.


Technical expertise

Name up to 4 people you go to for technical or operational advice and solutions. These people help you solve problems and get things done efficiently or creatively because of their expertise.

News and Information

Name up to 4 people you go to for information about new opportunities, events, or happenings at work or in your profession.

Political Support

Name up to 4 people you rely on to help you secure resources, positions, or opportunities to do new things or meet new people because of their role or influence.

Guidance

Name up to 4 people you go to for wisdom and feedback to help you reflect and change perspectives on important professional and personal questions. You value them as mentors or sounding boards.

Inspiration

Name up to 4 people who give you inspiration (energy, confidence, purpose) at work. They inspire, challenge, or encourage you to strive toward important goals .

Friendship

Name up to 4 people who have been the most important friends. You can discuss personal or non-professional matters with them. They give you emotional support and make you feel accepted.
